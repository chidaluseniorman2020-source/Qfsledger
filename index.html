Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Helpful notes about this QFS Ledger homepage prototype.

Is this a live cryptocurrency wallet?

No. This is a front-end website prototype. It does not create accounts, hold private keys, connect to a blockchain or process cryptocurrency transactions.

Are the balances and market prices real?

No. All balances, chart values and prices shown in this design are sample content for layout demonstration only.

Can visitors send or receive cryptocurrency now?

No. Those controls are previews only. Real wallet functions require audited integrations, secure key management, appropriate disclosures and testing before launch.

Will this page work on a phone?

Yes. The page is designed to adapt to smaller screens, with a mobile navigation menu and stacked content sections.
